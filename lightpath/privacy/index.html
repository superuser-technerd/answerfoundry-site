Lightpath Privacy Policy

Lightpath is built to work on your phone, not on our servers. This policy explains exactly what the app touches and what leaves your device.

Effective date: September 12, 2026

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Applies to: the Lightpath iOS app.

Lightpath is built to work on your phone, not on our servers. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising SDKs.

What we collect

Nothing. Lightpath does not collect, transmit or store personal information.

Location

When you allow it, Lightpath reads your device’s location to compute sun, moon and Milky Way positions for where you are. This happens on your device. Your location is not sent to us or stored by us. You can use the app without location access by picking a saved spot.

Camera

AR sky view shows your camera feed with sky markers drawn over it. The video is displayed live and is never recorded or transmitted. If you tap the capture button, that one frame is saved to your photo library with the overlay drawn on it — and nowhere else.

Weather and air quality

To show cloud cover, the app requests a forecast for your approximate coordinates from Apple Weather (WeatherKit), governed by Apple’s privacy policy. Air-quality data is requested from Open-Meteo using your approximate coordinates; no identifier is sent with the request.

Notifications

Return Alerts are scheduled locally on your device.

Recipes you save

Recipes are stored on your device, and in your iCloud backup if you have backups enabled. Sharing a recipe sends only the text you see on the card, through the app you choose.

Children

Lightpath does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
