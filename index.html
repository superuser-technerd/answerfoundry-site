The search shift

Search didn't die. It moved into the answer box.

AI assistants now answer buying questions directly — "best CPA near me," "who does kitchen remodels in MetroWest," "top Orlando immigration attorney." If AI engines can't find, verify, and trust your business, they recommend someone else. Most local businesses have no idea what AI says about them.

AI answers are winner-take-most

An AI response names a handful of businesses — not pages of results. Either you're in the answer, or you're invisible.

Your AI reputation already exists

AI engines are already describing your business from reviews, directories, and your website — accurately or not.

Early movers compound

Citations, structured data, and authoritative mentions accumulate. Businesses that optimize now become the default answer in their category.

The wake-up call

85/100 on SEO. Invisible to AI.

Kenny Chantasi is a business owner, not a marketer. When he ran his own company's website through a standard SEO audit, it scored 85 out of 100: fast, mobile-friendly, technically sound by every classic metric. Then he did what his own customers actually do — he asked ChatGPT and Google AI who the best provider near him was. His business never came up. Competitors with weaker websites did. That gap between looking good to Google and being invisible to AI is what he built AnswerFoundry to close — and it turns out the gap has nothing to do with what industry you're in.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Is this just SEO with a new name?

They overlap but aren't the same. Classic SEO earns rankings in a list of links. Answer engine optimization earns citations inside AI-generated answers — which depends on structured data, entity consistency, third-party corroboration, and content written so a machine can quote it confidently. We do both, because AI engines still lean heavily on search signals.

What's the difference between the free Snapshot and the paid Audit?

The Snapshot is a quick, limited check — enough to know if there's a real gap worth investigating. The Foundry Audit is the full diagnosis across every platform, with a prioritized 90-day plan and a call to walk through it.

Can you guarantee my business shows up in ChatGPT?

No — and you should be skeptical of anyone who guarantees placement in systems they don't control. What we can do is measure exactly where you stand, fix every signal within your control, and show you the movement month over month.

How fast will I see results?

The audit gives you the truth in about two weeks. Visibility improvements typically build over one to two quarters as AI engines re-crawl and re-weigh sources — local categories with weak competition often move faster.

Do you work outside Orlando?

Orlando and Central Florida are home base, and local businesses are our specialty. We take remote engagements across Florida when the fit is right.

Do you only work with medical practices?

No. We work with high-value local and professional service businesses across industries — law firms, home services and contractors, financial and accounting practices, real estate, healthcare, and others. Healthcare is one vertical we know well because it's where the founder first hit this problem, not the limit of who we serve.

Who's behind AnswerFoundry?

AnswerFoundry LLC is an Orlando-based consultancy founded by Kenny Chantasi, built on a simple premise: the businesses that win the next decade are the ones AI engines trust enough to recommend. Kenny is a physician by training and runs a medical practice — that's where he first ran into this problem — but AnswerFoundry is a marketing and AI-visibility firm that works across industries. It does not provide medical, legal, financial, or tax advice of any kind.
