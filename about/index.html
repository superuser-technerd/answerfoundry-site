About AnswerFoundry

Built by a business owner it was invisible to.

Kenny Chantasi founded AnswerFoundry after discovering that conventional SEO metrics said nothing about whether AI systems recommended his own business. Today AnswerFoundry does that testing and repair work for service businesses across Florida — in whatever industry they operate.

The wake-up call

85 out of 100 on SEO. Not once in AI's answer.

His site scored 85 out of 100 on a standard SEO audit — fast, mobile-friendly, technically sound by every classic metric. Then he asked ChatGPT and Google AI who the best provider near him was. His business never came up. Competitors with weaker websites did.

He built a structured process to find out why, and closed the gap for his own business first. That process turned out to have nothing to do with his industry. The same failure pattern — strong classic SEO, zero presence in AI answers — shows up in law firms, contractors, accounting practices, brokerages, and every other category where customers ask AI who to hire. AnswerFoundry exists to close that gap for them.

Why this matters now

The answer box replaced the results page

Customers increasingly ask AI systems for recommendations instead of scrolling a list of search results. An AI answer names a handful of businesses, not ten blue links — either you're in the answer, or you're not. Most local businesses have no idea what AI currently says about them, whether it's accurate, or which competitors are getting recommended instead.

What makes us different

We don't just report the gap. We close it.

We don't just report that a business is invisible to AI and hand over a dashboard. We identify the specific sources and signals influencing AI-generated answers, then do the implementation work needed to improve them — technical fixes, structured data, entity consistency, citation building, and content built to be quoted with confidence.

High-value local and professional service businesses across Florida

If your customers make a considered, high-stakes choice about who to hire — and increasingly start that research with an AI assistant — you are who we build for. We work across industries, not just one. Home base is Orlando and Central Florida, with remote engagements across the state.
