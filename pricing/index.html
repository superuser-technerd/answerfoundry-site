FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Is AnswerFoundry software?

No. AnswerFoundry uses technology to measure AI visibility, but the core offering is a professional service. Depending on the plan, we analyze your market, diagnose visibility gaps, implement website and structured-data improvements, optimize content, correct entity and listing inconsistencies, research authority opportunities, and monitor the results.

Why does Forge & Monitor cost more than an AI-tracking tool?

Tracking tools primarily show what happened. Forge & Monitor includes human analysis and defined implementation work intended to improve the underlying signals — not just another dashboard for your team to interpret and act on.

Do you guarantee results?

No — and you should be skeptical of anyone who guarantees placement in AI systems they don't control. What we guarantee is the work: every signal within your control gets tested, fixed, and tracked.

What's the difference between the Free Snapshot and the Foundry Audit?

The Snapshot is a quick, limited check — enough to know if there's a real gap. The Audit is the full diagnosis: every platform, every competitor, a prioritized 90-day plan, and a call to walk through it with you.

What is the commitment for each plan?

The Foundry Audit and Fix Sprint are one-time. Visibility Monitor is month-to-month with 30 days' notice. Forge & Monitor begins with a three-month initial implementation period and then continues month-to-month. Category Leader terms are stated in the written proposal, with a six-month initial term recommended for category-level work.

Are advertising, publication, directory, or other third-party costs included?

No, unless a written proposal expressly includes them. AnswerFoundry will obtain approval before incurring separately billable third-party costs.

Can I cancel anytime?

Visibility Monitor may be cancelled from the start with 30 days' written notice. Forge & Monitor may be cancelled after its initial three-month term with 30 days' written notice. Category Leader follows the initial term in its proposal.

What if I want a refund on the Foundry Audit?

The Audit is non-refundable once delivered — it's a completed diagnostic report, not an ongoing service. If you cancel before any work begins, a refund may be issued at AnswerFoundry's discretion.

Are there any hidden fees?

No setup fees beyond what's listed above. If a project needs something outside scope — a full website rebuild, for example — we'll tell you before any work starts, not after.

How is this different from SEO?

They overlap but aren't the same. SEO earns you a spot in a list of links. Answer engine optimization earns you a spot inside the answer itself — which depends on structured data, entity consistency, and content built to be quoted with confidence. We do both, because AI engines still lean on search signals.

What do you need from me to get started?

Access to your website (or your web developer's contact), your Google Business Profile, and 20 minutes for a discovery call. That's it to start the audit.
