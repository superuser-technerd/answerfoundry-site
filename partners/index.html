The Client Problem

A gap in scope, not a gap in your work

Most of your clients built their online presence around search engines. That work still matters — but a growing share of the questions that used to go to Google now go to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI Overviews instead. "Who's a good [service] in Orlando?" is increasingly answered by an AI model, not a search results page.

That's a different problem than the one most agencies are set up to solve. AI answer engines don't rank pages the way search engines do — they synthesize an answer from whatever sources they trust, and they cite very few of them. A client can have a well-optimized website and still be invisible in that answer.

This usually isn't a gap in your work. It's a gap in scope. Your clients likely haven't asked you about it directly, but a growing number are starting to notice they don't come up when they ask AI tools about their own industry — and they'll assume it's something you should already be handling. That creates a choice: build AI-visibility diagnostics and monitoring in-house, ignore the gap, or offer it through a partner who already does this work.

FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers

Do I need to know anything about AI/SEO to refer clients?

No. For a referral, you're simply introducing a client — AnswerFoundry handles the conversation, diagnosis, and delivery. For white-label options, we walk you through how to present findings in plain language your clients already understand.

What if my client already has an SEO or marketing retainer with me?

AI-visibility work is complementary, not competitive, with traditional SEO. Most partners position it as an addition to an existing retainer rather than a replacement for anything you currently do.

Can I resell this under my own brand name entirely?

Yes — that's the white-label audit and white-label implementation options. AnswerFoundry does the diagnostic and technical work; your brand is the only one the client sees.

How fast can a new referral get started?

Typically within a few business days of intake. The Free Answer Snapshot can often be turned around faster if you need something quick to show a prospective client.

What if I want to test this with one client before committing to a structure?

That's a reasonable way to start. Many partners begin with a single referral or a white-label audit for one client, then decide which structure fits their business once they've seen how the work and the client conversation actually go.
