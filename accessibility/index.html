Accessibility Statement

We want this site to work for everyone. This page says honestly where we are, not where we would like you to think we are.

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Last reviewed: August 8, 2026

Target standard: WCAG 2.2 Level AA

Conformance status: Partially conformant — self-assessed, not independently audited. See Section 3.

1. Our commitment

AnswerFoundry LLC is committed to making answerfoundry.ai usable by people with disabilities, including people who use screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, magnification, voice control, or other assistive technology. We treat accessibility as part of building the site, not as a separate project.

2. The standard we target

We target the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium. WCAG 2.2 has been the current W3C Recommendation since October 2023 and was adopted as ISO/IEC 40500 in 2025.

We target 2.2 rather than the earlier 2.1 because it is the current version and includes criteria — focus appearance, target size, dragging alternatives, and reducing redundant entry among them — that matter for a site built around forms.

3. Where we actually stand

This site has not been independently audited. The conformance claim above is our own assessment, based on automated testing and manual keyboard and screen-reader checks. We are stating this plainly rather than publishing a conformance claim we have not earned. An independent audit is planned; when it is complete, this page will say so and will report what it found.

What we have done:

  • Semantic HTML with a single main landmark, a logical heading order, and named navigation regions;
  • A skip-to-content link on every page;
  • Visible, high-contrast focus indicators on every interactive element;
  • Text and interface contrast checked against the 4.5:1 and 3:1 thresholds;
  • Full keyboard operability, with no keyboard traps;
  • Form fields with programmatically associated labels, explicit required-field indication, and errors identified in text rather than by color alone;
  • Content that reflows without horizontal scrolling at 320 CSS pixels and at 200% zoom;
  • Text spacing that tolerates user overrides;
  • Touch and pointer targets sized and spaced to at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels;
  • No text embedded in images used to convey page content — the only images on these pages are the AnswerFoundry logo mark, which is decorative and sits beside the text name, and social-preview cards that are never rendered on the page itself;
  • No automatically playing media and no content that flashes;
  • No externally hosted webfonts and minimal script, which reduces the ways the page can fail.

4. Known and likely limitations

Being specific is more useful than a blanket claim:

  • The chat widget. Our website chat is provided by a third party (HighLevel / LeadConnector). We do not control its markup and have not verified its keyboard or screen-reader behavior. If it is a barrier for you, every other way of reaching us in Section 6 works just as well and gets the same response.
  • Blog and older marketing pages. Pages published before this statement have not all been re-checked against WCAG 2.2. We are working through them.
  • Third-party content. Documents, embeds, or tools from other providers may not meet the same standard.
  • PDF deliverables. Reports we send to clients are not currently produced as tagged, accessible PDFs. If you need a deliverable in an accessible format — tagged PDF, plain HTML, or plain text — ask and we will provide it at no additional charge.

5. How we assess

We use a combination of automated testing tools, manual keyboard-only navigation, screen-reader spot checks, zoom and reflow testing, and contrast measurement. Automated tools catch a minority of accessibility problems; we do not treat a clean automated scan as conformance, and neither should anyone reading this page.

6. Tell us about a barrier

If any part of this site is difficult or impossible for you to use, we want to hear about it — and we would rather hear it directly than have you give up. Use whichever of these is easiest for you:

  • Email: hello@answerfoundry.ai — put "Accessibility" in the subject line if you can, but it is not required.
  • Web form: our contact page, which has an "Accessibility" reason option.
  • Postal mail: AnswerFoundry LLC, 2445 S Hiawassee Rd, PMB 1021, Orlando, FL 32835.

Tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Your browser and assistive technology help but are not required.

Our response commitment: we acknowledge accessibility reports within 5 business days and tell you what we plan to do and roughly when. If a fix will take time, we will offer another way to get what you needed in the meantime.

7. Getting the same result another way

Anything you can do on this site, you can do by email or postal mail instead. That includes requesting a free Answer Snapshot, asking about pricing, applying to the partner program, and making a privacy request. We will not ask you to explain why.

8. A note about our services

Accessibility remediation of client websites is not included in our services and we do not audit client sites for accessibility conformance. Where we notice something during our work, we will flag it and can recommend a specialist. We build our own deliverables to the standard described here.

9. Updates to this statement

We review this page at least annually and whenever we make a significant change to the site. The review date at the top is real, not automatic.
