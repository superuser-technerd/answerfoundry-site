Cookie Policy

This page lists every cookie and third-party script answerfoundry.ai actually loads. It is a factual inventory, not a general description.

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Inventory last verified: August 8, 2026

Our standing rule: this page is updated the same day any tag, script, pixel, or analytics tool is added to or removed from this site.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. Related technologies — local storage, pixels, and scripts loaded from another domain — can serve similar purposes. This policy covers all of them, and uses "cookies" as shorthand.

2. What this site actually loads

Below is the complete current inventory. Every third-party item on this site comes from our customer relationship management and chat vendor, HighLevel Inc., which operates under the LeadConnector and msgsndr domains.

2.3 Hosting

Our site is hosted by Netlify, Inc., which handles form submissions and keeps standard server logs including IP addresses. Netlify does not set advertising or analytics cookies on this site.

3. What this site does not load

As of the verification date above, this site contains no:

  • Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager;
  • Microsoft Clarity or any other session-replay tool;
  • Meta (Facebook) pixel, or any advertising network tag or retargeting pixel;
  • Google Fonts or any other externally hosted webfont;
  • Calendly or any other embedded scheduling widget;
  • Any image, font, script, or style loaded from a domain we do not control — the logo mark, icons, and social-preview cards on these policy pages are served from answerfoundry.ai itself and set no cookies;
  • A/B testing, heatmapping, or behavioral profiling tool of any kind.

We state this because it is true today and because it is unusual. If we add any of these, this page changes the same day and this list shrinks accordingly.

4. Consent banner

We do not display a cookie consent banner. The only cookie this site sets is a strictly necessary bot-management cookie, and we run no advertising or analytics tags, so consent is not required under the laws that apply to us. If we add any cookie that is not strictly necessary, we will add a consent mechanism before that cookie is set, and update this page.

5. How to control cookies

  • Browser settings. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies. Blocking the bot-management cookie may prevent the chat widget from loading; the rest of the site will work normally.
  • Do not use the chat widget. The widget is what loads the third-party resources above. If you would rather not load them, email hello@answerfoundry.ai instead — that reaches us just as well.
  • Global Privacy Control. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, so a GPC signal has nothing to opt you out of on this site. We honor it regardless.

6. Related

What we do with the information itself, including the vendors named above, is described in our Privacy Policy. Text messaging is covered in our SMS Program Terms.

7. Found something we missed?

If you see a script or cookie on this site that is not listed here, tell us at hello@answerfoundry.ai and we will fix the page. We would rather be corrected than be wrong.
