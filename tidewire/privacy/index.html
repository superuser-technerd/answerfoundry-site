Tidewire Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Tidewire is a network utility for iPhone and iPad that routes your device's traffic to proxy servers you configure. It is published by Kenny Chantasi.

What we collect

Nothing. Tidewire has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no servers of its own. We do not receive any information about you or your use of the app.

Where your data lives

Server addresses, routing rules, and settings are stored on your device in the app's private container. Passwords are stored in the iOS Keychain. None of this is transmitted to us.

Network traffic

When connected, your traffic is sent to the proxy server you selected. The operator of that server can see the traffic that reaches it. We do not operate, endorse, or have access to any server you add. You are responsible for choosing servers you trust.

Subscriptions

If you add a subscription URL, the app downloads that URL when you refresh it. That request goes to the URL's operator, not to us.

Diagnostics

Tunnel logging is off by default. When you turn it on, logs are written to the app's container on your device and are never uploaded. You can delete them from the app at any time.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan server QR codes, only while the scanner screen is open. No images are stored or transmitted.

Children

Tidewire is a technical tool intended for adults managing their own network configuration.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
