Does Perplexity need a different AI visibility strategy than ChatGPT and Google?

Ask Perplexity and ChatGPT the same question about your business and you can get two different answers, built from two different sets of sources. That's not a glitch. Perplexity searches the web live on every query and cites what it finds in that moment, while ChatGPT leans more on what it already knows. The difference is real and documented, and it means a visibility plan tuned only for ChatGPT can leave gaps in how Perplexity finds and describes you.

What this means

According to Perplexity's own documentation, the platform works by interpreting a question, searching the web in real time for information from "authoritative sources like articles, websites, and journals," then compiling what it finds into an answer with numbered citations back to the original pages (Perplexity, 2026). That's a retrieval-first design: every answer is built from a fresh search, not primarily from what the model already "knows" from training. Perplexity's Pro Search and Research modes take this further, running multiple searches per question and, in Research mode, reading through hundreds of sources before writing a report.

That design choice matters for visibility. It means Perplexity is, by construction, more dependent on what's crawlable and citable about your business right now than an engine leaning more heavily on pretrained knowledge. If your information online is thin, outdated, or inconsistent, a live-search engine simply has less to work with at the moment it's answering.

Who this applies to

Any business already running an AI visibility strategy for ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews should ask whether Perplexity needs separate attention, particularly local and professional-service businesses where "best [service] near me" questions are common, and B2B companies, since research on Perplexity's citation patterns shows it leaning more heavily on community and review platforms for those specific query types.

How we'd evaluate this

A proper comparison runs the same test question across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews, then looks specifically at which sources each one cites, not just whether your business gets named. If Perplexity cites a directory listing, review platform, or forum thread that ChatGPT ignores, that's a real signal about where each engine is pulling its confidence from, and where a gap in your presence would hurt one platform more than another.

Available options

There are three ways to act on what you find:

  • Run the DIY comparison and stop there. Free and directional: ask the same question on all three engines and write down who and what got cited.
  • Book a full AI visibility audit. Maps the specific gaps, inconsistent listings, thin directory presence, missing structured data, and prioritizes what to fix first, engine by engine.
  • Ongoing monitoring. Citation patterns shift as these platforms update their retrieval and ranking logic. Monthly tracking catches drift before a competitor quietly takes the slot you had.

Benefits, limitations, and what this won't tell you

The benefit of understanding how Perplexity works is that it points to concrete, checkable things: is your Google Business Profile complete, are your directory listings accurate, is your review presence current, since these show up repeatedly in independent research on what Perplexity cites. The limitation is that no published research, including Perplexity's own documentation, discloses a precise ranking formula. Third-party studies of what gets cited don't always agree with each other either, because they use different query sets, industries, and time windows, sometimes just months apart, and these platforms change quickly.

What we know about how Perplexity cites sources

Two independently conducted citation studies, both reported by Search Engine Land, illustrate the size of the gap and how much it depends on the type of question being asked. An analysis of 30 million cited sources across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews found Reddit was the single most-cited domain across all five engines, with Perplexity specifically leaning on Reddit, LinkedIn, and G2 for B2B queries, a different mix than ChatGPT (Wikipedia, Reddit, editorial sites like Forbes) or Google (Facebook, Yelp) (Goodwin, 2026).

A separate analysis of 6.8 million citations across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity, focused specifically on consumer, location-based queries, found a different emphasis: 86% of citations overall came from sources brands already control, first-party websites and directory listings, with forums accounting for just 2%. In that dataset, Perplexity's citations spread across sources like MapQuest and TripAdvisor rather than concentrating on one source type the way Gemini leaned on websites or ChatGPT leaned on listings (Goodwin, 2025).

Read together, the two studies don't contradict each other so much as measure different things: general informational queries appear to pull more community and editorial sources, while local recommendation queries pull more from directories and listings a business can directly manage. Perplexity shows up differently in both datasets, never dominated by a single source type the way individual engines were in each study.

Next steps

Run your existing AI visibility test on Perplexity specifically, using a "best [service] near [city]" phrasing, and write down which sources it cites alongside its answer, not just whether it named you. That citation list tells you where to focus first, and whether the gap is on your website, your directory listings, or your review presence.

Orlando considerations

Central Florida is a dense, competitive market in categories like med spas, dental practices, law firms, and home services, often with several similarly positioned businesses within a few miles of each other. Because Perplexity's citations lean on directory and listing sources for local queries, incomplete or inconsistent listings across platforms like Google Business Profile, Yelp, and industry-specific directories create more room for a competitor to get cited instead, simply because their information was easier to verify.

Frequently asked questions

Does Perplexity use the same ranking factors as Google?

No. Perplexity's own documentation describes a retrieval-first process: it searches the web in real time for every question and builds its answer from what it finds in that moment, with numbered citations back to the sources. Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT draw more heavily on their own indexes and pretrained knowledge. The practical effect is that what's crawlable and current about your business matters more, more often, for Perplexity.

Is Reddit really the most important platform for Perplexity?

It depends on the type of question. A 30-million-source study found Reddit was the most-cited domain across every major AI engine, including Perplexity. A separate study focused specifically on local, recommendation-style queries found the opposite pattern: 86% of citations came from brand-controlled sources like websites and directory listings, with forums at just 2%. Both are real findings from real research, they measured different kinds of questions.

Can I guarantee my business gets cited by Perplexity?

No, and you should be skeptical of anyone who says otherwise. Perplexity doesn't publish its exact citation algorithm, and no outside party controls what it decides to cite. What's achievable is making sure your website, listings, and reviews are accurate and consistent, since those are the levers every study points back to.

Should I run a separate AI visibility campaign just for Perplexity?

Not a separate campaign, but separate testing. The underlying fixes, accurate business information, complete directory and review presence, structured content, benefit every AI engine. What changes is what you check: for Perplexity specifically, test with a live query and look at which sources it cites, since that's the clearest signal of what it currently trusts about your category.
